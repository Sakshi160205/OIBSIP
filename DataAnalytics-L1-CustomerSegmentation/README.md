# Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this project is to segment customers based on their purchasing behaviour using **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis** and **K-Means clustering**.

Customer segmentation helps businesses understand different customer groups and develop targeted marketing strategies.

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Scikit-learn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

The following steps were performed as part of the analysis:

- Data loading and inspection
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- RFM analysis
- Recency calculation
- Frequency calculation
- Monetary value calculation
- RFM scoring
- Feature scaling using `StandardScaler`
- Determining the optimal number of clusters using the Elbow Method
- K-Means clustering
- Cluster visualization
- Customer segment profiling
- Business and marketing recommendations

## RFM Analysis

RFM analysis evaluates customers based on three important metrics:

- **Recency:** How recently a customer made a purchase
- **Frequency:** How frequently a customer makes purchases
- **Monetary:** How much money a customer spends

These metrics are used to understand customer value and behaviour.

## Customer Segmentation

K-Means clustering is applied to the scaled RFM features to group customers with similar purchasing behaviour.

The **Elbow Method** is used to determine a suitable number of clusters.

## Key Insights

Insights will be added after completing the analysis.

The analysis will identify:

- High-value customers
- Loyal and frequent customers
- Recent customers
- Customers with low purchasing activity
- Customers who may require re-engagement
- Differences in spending behaviour across customer segments

## Business Recommendations

Based on the identified customer segments, suitable marketing strategies can include:

- Providing loyalty rewards to high-value customers
- Offering personalized promotions to frequent customers
- Sending re-engagement campaigns to inactive customers
- Providing targeted discounts to customers with low purchasing frequency
- Developing personalized marketing campaigns for different customer segments

Final recommendations will be updated based on the clustering results.
